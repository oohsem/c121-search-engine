CS121 Project 3
Milestone 3 evaluation queries

Method

The fixed set contains 10 initially weak and 10 initially strong queries.
The starting point was log-TF cosine with query IDF. The strong group had
at least three relevant results in its first five. The same Developer
corpus, query intents, and unique-URL rule were used before and after tuning. Offline
judgments were based on indexed URLs, titles, headings, and page text.
P@5 is the fraction of the first five results judged relevant. Evaluation
timed query receipt through result assembly, excluding display.

Changes and observed effects

The final ranker prefers complete matches and fills remaining slots with
partial matches. It uses selective query-time stopping, important HTML
weighting, and a soft penalty for very short pages. The short-page penalty
helped keep image-only and directory pages from outranking substantive
machine-learning resources. The same scoring rules apply to every query.

Across this offline set, P@5 rose from 0.43 to 0.66. Seven of the ten
initially weak queries improved. The other three retained their scores.
All ten initially strong queries retained or improved their scores.

The 20 queries were timed four times each on the full index. The median
was 20.4 ms and the slowest run was 52.3 ms. Every timed run met 300 ms.
